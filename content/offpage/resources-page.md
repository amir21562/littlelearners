## Helpful resources for UK parents of 3–6 year olds

Learning doesn't have to mean subscriptions or screen time. These are the free
and official resources we point parents to most — alongside our own printable
packs.

### Understanding the curriculum

- **GOV.UK — Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS)**
  The official framework every nursery and Reception class in England follows.
  If you want to know what "school readiness" actually means, start here:
  gov.uk/government/publications/early-years-foundation-stage-framework--2

- **BBC Bitesize — Early Years**
  Free games and videos matched to the EYFS, made by the BBC:
  bbc.co.uk/bitesize/levels/z3g4d2p

### Phonics

- **Twinkl — Free Phonics Resources**
  The UK's biggest teacher-resource site; its free section includes Phase 2
  sound mats and flashcards: twinkl.co.uk (search "phase 2 phonics", filter: free)

- **PhonicsPlay (free section)**
  Interactive phonics games many UK schools use in class — good for 10 minutes
  of tablet time that actually teaches: phonicsplay.co.uk

- **Our guide: Phase 2 Sounds in Order**
  The full Letters & Sounds Phase 2 sequence with pronunciation tips for parents:
  littlelearners.store/guides/phase-2-sounds-in-order *(publish with guides section)*

### Early maths

- **NRICH Early Years (University of Cambridge)**
  Free, research-backed maths activities for young children:
  nrich.maths.org/early-years

- **White Rose Maths — 1-Minute Maths app**
  A free app schools recommend for quick daily number practice:
  whiterosemaths.com

### Fine motor skills & writing

- **NHS — Physical activity guidelines for under-5s**
  Official guidance on the movement and play that builds writing muscles:
  nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/physical-activity-guidelines-children-under-five-years

### Parenting support

- **Mumsnet — Primary Education Talk**
  The UK's largest parenting community; honest threads on starting school and
  helping at home: mumsnet.com/talk/primary

- **Netmums**
  Articles and local parent meetups across the UK: netmums.com
