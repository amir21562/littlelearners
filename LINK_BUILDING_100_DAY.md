# 100-Day Backlink & Authority Plan

Goal: earn real, relevant links to the 4 guides and the shop. No buying links,
no link farms, no automated spam — one penalty wipes out months of content work.

## Principles

- Relevance beats volume: a link from a UK parenting blog beats 50 directory links.
- Link to **guides**, not just the shop — useful content earns links; product
  pages rarely do.
- Every link must be defensible if Google (or a parent) looks at it.

## Days 1–14 — Foundations (profiles & citations)

- [ ] Create/complete profiles with a website link: Facebook Page, Instagram,
      Pinterest (big for printables), TikTok, YouTube channel.
- [ ] UK business citations: Google Business Profile (if applicable), Bing
      Places, Yell, FreeIndex, Scoot — consistent name/address/URL.
- [ ] Submit the site to 3–5 curated education/parenting resource lists that
      accept submissions (check each list's rules first).

## Days 15–40 — Community & Web 2.0

- [ ] Answer parent questions on Mumsnet, Netmums and relevant subreddits
      (r/UKParenting etc.) where a guide genuinely answers the question — link
      the guide, not the shop; disclose affiliation. Read each community's
      self-promotion rules first.
- [ ] Publish genuinely useful companion content on Medium / a company blog
      presence (e.g. "How I taught my 4-year-old pure sounds in 5 minutes a
      day") linking back to the relevant guide.
- [ ] Comment thoughtfully on UK parenting/teacher blogs (name + site URL in
      the profile field, never keyword-stuffed anchor text).

## Days 41–70 — Outreach (guest posts & niche edits)

- [ ] List 20 UK parenting, homeschooling and teacher blogs. Pitch 3 guest
      post ideas tailored to each (e.g. "5 phonics mistakes parents make" for
      a parenting blog) — one contextual link to the matching guide.
- [ ] Niche edits: find existing articles about phonics/number formation/
      school readiness that link to thin or dead resources; email the author
      suggesting our guide as a better, parent-friendly reference.
- [ ] Offer the free sample PDF to 5–10 parenting resource roundups.

## Days 71–100 — Podcasts, partnerships & review

- [ ] Pitch 5 UK parenting/education podcasts as a guest ("what schools wish
      parents knew about phonics") — show notes link to the guides hub.
- [ ] Partner with 2–3 micro-influencers (UK mum/teacher accounts) — gifted
      bundle in exchange for an honest review post with a link.
- [ ] Review: log every link earned (URL, date, type). Disavow nothing unless
      Search Console shows a manual action — just stop anything that looks spammy.

## What NOT to do

- Don't buy links, join link exchanges at scale, or use automated link tools.
- Don't put keyword-rich anchor text everywhere — branded/natural anchors
  ("LittleLearners' phase 2 guide") look real because they are real.
- Don't link the same page from the same domain twice — one good link per
  relationship.
